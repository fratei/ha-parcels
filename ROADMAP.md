# Roadmap

What's planned for Parcels after the 0.1 bug-fix releases, by milestone. Each item links to its issue, where the details are. The order may change, and comments are welcome in the issues.

Size: S small, M medium, L large.

## Not planned

- Delivery photos: Parcels keeps no photos, and carriers' photos need their logins.
- Signing in to Amazon for "stops away": it needs an unofficial login.
- Scraping carriers' websites: it can break their terms. Community integrations that do it can be read through the community card compatibility item.
- Paid tracking APIs (Ship24, AfterShip) as sources.
- Price-drop alerts, carbon footprint, receipts and spending: these need the cloud or scraping, or go beyond deliveries.
- Reading SMS: Android only, and heavy on privacy.
